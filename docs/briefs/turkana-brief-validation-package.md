## 5. Feedback Call Guide

### Opening

Thanks for taking time to review this. This is an early validation draft, so honest feedback is more useful than approval.

The main question is whether this brief helps county or partner teams think more clearly about facility access, service gaps, and planning priorities.

### Questions

1. What planning decisions are you currently involved in or preparing for?
2. Does this brief answer any questions relevant to that work?
3. Which section is most useful?
4. Which section is least useful?
5. What data is missing?
6. Are the facility counts and ownership mix believable from your experience?
7. Are the operational status and bed capacity figures useful?
8. Which services should be added first: ART, FP, IPD, maternal health, emergency referral, or something else?
9. Would this brief be useful quarterly, annually, or only during planning cycles?
10. Who else should review this?

### Closing

Thanks. I’ll use this feedback to improve the next version.

Would it be okay if I follow up after revising the brief?

## 7. Success Target

For the first validation round, the goal is not mass distribution.

The goal is:

* 5 targeted outreach messages sent
* 2–3 real feedback conversations
* 1 person saying whether they would use this in planning
* Clear list of missing data or format improvements
* At least 1 referral to another useful reviewer
